**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cyrusshepard_new-how-ai-overviews-shift-traffic-from-activity-7326294369819340801-uER1
**Summary:** Cyrus shares his Zyppy study showing that many links inside Google's AI Overviews route users back into further Google searches rather than to the original source websites, and cites third-party research (Amsive, Ahrefs, Seer Interactive) documenting double-digit percentage click-through-rate declines on queries that trigger AI Overviews.
**Key Takeaways**
