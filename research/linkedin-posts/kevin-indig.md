# LinkedIn Posts

## Post 1
**Title:** How AI Content Backfires
**Publication Date:** Approximate October 2025
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_how-ai-content-backfires-activity-7375911704481873920-whHr
**Summary:** Post examining cases where AI-generated or AI-scaled content produced measurable ranking or trust damage rather than growth, aligning with Kevin's broader research thesis that content freshness, specificity, and verifiable signals matter more to AI systems than volume.
**Key Takeaways**
- Scaling content production with AI does not automatically translate into better AI search visibility.
- Content quality and verifiability signals matter more to LLMs than sheer publishing volume.
- Backfire risk is a recurring theme worth tracking systematically, not a one-off anecdote.

## Post 2
**Title:** Top of Mind Matters More Than Top Ranking
**Publication Date:** Approximate late January 2026
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_top-of-mind-matters-more-than-top-ranking-activity-7420806512761040896-2Qx_
**Summary:** Argues that in an AI-answer-driven search environment, brand recognition and being the entity users already trust matters more than classic keyword-ranking position, echoing Kevin's public research on trust-first filtering behavior in AI Overviews.
**Key Takeaways**
- Users apply a "trust first, then relevance" filter when evaluating AI-generated answers.
- Brand familiarity increasingly outweighs precise on-page keyword optimization as a ranking-equivalent signal.
- Content strategy should be built to reinforce recognizable, trusted brand presence, not just target keywords.

## Post 3
**Title:** Trust-First Wins in AI Search
**Publication Date:** Approximate early February 2026
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_trust-first-wins-in-ai-search-activity-7425857050036924416-dBoE
**Summary:** Companion post to Kevin's trust-first research theme, reinforcing that AI search surfaces reward established, trusted sources over content that is merely well-optimized, with implications for how AI-produced content should be sourced and framed.
**Key Takeaways**
- Trust signals (recognized brand, third-party validation) increasingly precede relevance signals in AI search ranking behavior.
- AI-produced content benefits from being anchored to verifiable, trusted sources rather than generic optimization.
- This reinforces a broader shift from "traffic" to "influence" as the metric that matters in AI search.
