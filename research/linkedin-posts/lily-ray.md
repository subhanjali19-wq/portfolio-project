## LinkedIn Posts

### Post 1 — "My Bets on What the Next Google Core Algorithm Update Will Devalue"
- **Publication date**: ~October 16, 2025
- **URL**: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_my-bets-on-what-the-next-google-core-algorithm-activity-7382816896095764480-PnXi
- **Summary**: Drawing on 15+ years analyzing Google core updates, Ray predicted which content patterns Google would likely devalue next, based on ranking volatility she and other trackers observed in mid-October 2025. She specifically called out thin, robotic pages built to influence LLMs and explicit prompt-injection tactics aimed at generative engine optimization.
- **Key takeaways**:
  1. Google algorithm updates affect AI search visibility broadly, not just Google's own AI surfaces.
  2. Content produced primarily to manipulate LLM citations (rather than serve users) is a recurring target of devaluation.
  3. Experience-based pattern recognition across many past updates lets practitioners anticipate crackdowns before they're officially confirmed.

### Post 2 — "Is Google Finally Cracking Down on Self-Promotional Listicles?"
- **Publication date**: February 3, 2026
- **URL**: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_is-google-finally-cracking-down-on-self-promotional-activity-7424484657540321280-bxrU
- **Summary**: Ray identified a major pattern while auditing sites hit by recent Google volatility: businesses that scaled "best X" self-promotional listicles (ranking their own product/company first) were disproportionately losing visibility. She argued this once-effective GEO shortcut was starting to backfire.
- **Key takeaways**:
  1. A tactic can work well for months and still represent long-term SEO/AI-search risk.
  2. Self-promotional "best of" content is increasingly distinguishable — and penalizable — at scale.
  3. Objective, third-party roundup content continues to outperform first-party self-promotion in both organic and AI-generated results.

### Post 3 — "These Are 3 Well-Known Brands..." (AI Content Scaling Penalty)
- **Publication date**: June 8, 2026
- **URL**: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_these-are-3-well-known-brands-that-are-also-ugcPost-7469712876065189888-TQuT/
- **Summary**: Ray shared SISTRIX visibility data on three brands publicly cited as case studies by AI content-scaling tools — all three hit their lowest organic visibility in five-plus years following the January 2026 and subsequent core updates. The AI-generated content lived in a single subfolder on each site, yet the penalty affected the entire domain.
- **Key takeaways**:
  1. Google can penalize an entire domain even when problematic AI-generated content is isolated to one subfolder.
  2. Being showcased as an official "success story" by an AI content-scaling vendor is not a reliable signal of long-term safety.
  3. Scaled "best X" pages, comparison pages, and spammy structured data are a recurring, identifiable fingerprint of at-risk AI content strategies.
