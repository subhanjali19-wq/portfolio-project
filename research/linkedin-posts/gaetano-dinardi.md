### Post 2
**Title:** "Dark Social ➡️ Dark SEO" (companion LinkedIn post to his Substack/Search Engine Land article on the "Dark SEO Funnel")
**Publication Date:** Approximate (February–March 2026, coinciding with the related Search Engine Land article published February 27, 2026)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/dark-social-seo-gaetano-nino-dinardi-8flje
**Summary:** Gaetano introduces the concept of the "Dark SEO Funnel," describing how LLMs like ChatGPT and Perplexity recommend brands, after which users verify the brand via a branded Google search — with the credit misattributed to "Direct" or "Branded Search" traffic in analytics. He outlines proxy metrics marketers should track instead of raw organic traffic.
**Key Takeaways**
* AI-driven brand discovery is frequently invisible in traditional analytics because users "verify" AI recommendations via branded search, masking the original AI-driven touchpoint.
* Recommended proxy metrics include branded search volume, product/solutions page traffic, landing page conversion rates, and self-reported attribution citing "ChatGPT" or "Perplexity."
* Entity strength — built through PR, podcasts, reviews, and "surround sound SEO" — drives whether a brand gets explicitly recommended by an LLM.

### Post 3
**Title:** GEO is a reputation problem. Not a technical problem. 
**Publication Date:** Approximate April 2026
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/officialg_why-geo-is-a-reputation-problem-activity-7455228144887894016-uyRj/
**Summary:** Gaetano argues that Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is fundamentally a brand reputation and positioning challenge rather than a technical SEO exercise. He explains that commonly promoted GEO tactics—such as llms.txt, FAQ schema, AI information pages, and question-based headings—have limited impact on AI recommendations when compared with strong brand recognition, category alignment, and a consistent digital reputation. He encourages organizations to coordinate SEO, product marketing, PR, partnerships, and customer advocacy to strengthen how AI systems understand and recommend their brand.
**Key Takeaways**
* AI visibility depends more on reputation, positioning, and category authority than on isolated technical SEO tactics.
* Tactics such as llms.txt, FAQ schema, and AI-specific formatting have relatively small impact compared with building a trusted brand.
* Effective GEO requires collaboration across SEO, marketing, PR, partnerships, and customer advocacy to create consistent signals that AI systems can recognize and trust.
