## Post 3
**Title:** Is SEO Dead in 2026? — ChatGPT/Gemini Citation Research Findings
**Publication Date:** Approximate June 2026
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/michaelkingphilly_interesting-findings-specifically-around-activity-7475441861982203904-pOG7/
**Summary:** Mike shared findings from his TUM (Technical University of Munich) master's thesis analyzing how LLMs use web search when recommending products. Across ~1,650 runs on ChatGPT Plus, ChatGPT Business, and Gemini (79 prompts, 3 runs each, three model generations), he captured 370,000+ search results and 13,000+ citations, scoped to the AI speech/translation tools domain.
**Key Takeaways**
- ChatGPT's two tiers ground answers differently: Business reads Bing, while Plus reads scraped Google results — meaning "optimize for ChatGPT" is not a single target.
- Roughly 80% of AI product recommendations traced directly back to conventional search rank; on Bing-grounded results specifically, 94% of citations came from the top 10.
- About 12–15% of citations bypass search entirely through an internal "labrador" channel (Wikipedia, arXiv, major tech press) that standard SEO/GEO tactics can't influence — meaning a portion of AI visibility is structurally out of reach for content producers.
- His core conclusion: "good SEO is the bare minimum for good GEO" — reinforcing that AI content strategies can't substitute for underlying search fundamentals.
