## Summary
Marie Haynes' newsletter, "Search and AI News You Can Use," has run for 12 years and is her primary channel for tracking Google algorithm changes, AI Overviews and AI Mode developments, and practical SEO/GEO guidance. The free tier curates weekly industry news, while a paid tier ("Search Bar Pro," part of her broader "Search Bar" community) adds deeper strategic commentary, prompts and tutorials for using AI in SEO workflows, and twice-monthly live discussion sessions. The newsletter is tightly integrated with her podcast, "Search News You Can Use," which covers the same topics in long-form audio/video format.

Content spans Google core update analysis, DOJ trial disclosures about Google's ranking systems (e.g., Navboost, RankEmbed BERT), E-E-A-T and Quality Raters' Guidelines interpretation, and increasingly, coverage of agentic search, AI Search Console reporting, and Google's official generative-AI optimization documentation. The format blends rapid-response news coverage with Marie's own testing and client-based observations.

## Why This Resource Matters
As a long-running, high-frequency publication from a practitioner with over a decade of Google algorithm tracking experience, this newsletter is a primary, continuously updated source for understanding how AI-powered search is reshaping SEO content production. It is particularly valuable for AI-Powered SEO Content Production research because it consistently translates Google's official statements and technical documentation (RAG, query fan-out, non-commodity content) into concrete guidance for content creators and site owners.
