## A Reflection on SEO, GEO & AI Search in 2025

**Type**: Substack newsletter (long-form year-in-review)
**URL**: https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/a-reflection-on-seo-and-ai-search  
**Publication date**: January 20, 2026  

**Summary**: Ray's year-in-review piece covers the 2025 hype cycle around "SEO is dead," including coordinated GEO disinformation campaigns allegedly run by AI-tool vendors to promote that narrative. She shares a consistent personal observation: her own articles were reliably cited across LLMs within hours of publishing, using nothing more than standard SEO practices — because LLMs lean on search engines' existing indexing and ranking. She also covers the "vibe coding" boom (Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable) that made it trivial for non-developers to spin up AI-search-tracking tools, flooding the industry with competing point solutions.

**Why it's valuable for AI-powered SEO content production**: This piece is one of the clearest documented arguments that AI content visibility is not a separate discipline requiring exotic new tactics — it's largely downstream of solid, well-indexed, well-ranked SEO content. For a research project on AI-powered content production, it's a useful counterweight to vendor-driven "GEO hacks" framing, grounded in a full year of the author's own before/after observations rather than theory.
