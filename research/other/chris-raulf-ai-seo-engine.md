# My AI SEO Engine: How I Turn Sales Calls into Top Ranking Content

**Type:** Blog (chrisraulf.com)
**Publication Date:** April 14, 2026
**URL:** https://chrisraulf.com/my-ai-seo-engine-how-i-turn-sales-calls-into-top-ranking-results-in-google-and-large-language-models/

## Summary
In this blog post, Chris Raulf details the specific AI-driven content production system he built to convert raw, unscripted sales-call transcripts into fully optimized, top-ranking thought-leadership articles. The system relies on two core inputs: an "Expert Profile Module" (a "Virtual Chris" trained on his body of work to replicate his authentic voice) and "Project Parameters" (target website, audience, and the source transcript). Rather than starting with keyword research, the AI workflow first analyzes the full transcript to identify the single most strategically valuable theme — described as the "Big Idea" — that best showcases expertise and attracts high-value clients.

Once the theme is approved, the engine proceeds through a structured, multi-stage process: keyword strategy, outline construction, first-person drafting in his established voice and tone (avoiding hedging language and generic filler), automatic integration of a relevant existing YouTube video from his channel, and generation of 3–5 internal links (with target pages and anchor text) plus 3–5 external links to high-authority, non-competitive sources to strengthen E-E-A-T signals. The post positions this as a core component of his trademarked "Micro-SEO Strategies℠" methodology, developed after presenting an earlier version of the concept at DigiMarCon Denver 2025.

## Why This Resource Matters
This article is a concrete, step-by-step case study of an AI-powered SEO content production pipeline built and used in-house by a working SEO agency, rather than a theoretical or vendor-marketing description of AI content tools. It shows how a real practitioner structures AI prompting and workflow stages (theme identification → keyword strategy → drafting → internal/external linking → multimedia integration) to produce content designed to rank in both traditional Google search and large language model outputs. For a repository on AI-Powered SEO Content Production, it offers a transferable blueprint for combining a defined "expert voice" profile with a repeatable AI content pipeline.
