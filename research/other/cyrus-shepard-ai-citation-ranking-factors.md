## Summary
Cyrus Shepard's "AI Citation Ranking Factors Analysis" is a meta-analysis synthesizing 54 published experiments, patents, and case studies about what drives citations in AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, with reference to Google AI Overviews). Shepard collected nearly every available AI-citation study from the prior two years, narrowed them to the 54 most useful sources, and scored 23 recurring factors on a 0–10 scale based on repeatability across studies, strength of evidence, and official support from platform documentation or patents.

The top-scoring factors were URL accessibility (9.5), search rank (9.4), fan-out rank (9.3), preview control (9.2), and query-answer match (9.2) — all closely tied to traditional SEO fundamentals such as crawlability, ranking strength, and content structure. Lower-scoring factors included domain authority (5.0) and LLMs.txt (2.0), the latter of which Shepard found no credible evidence actually influences AI citations. The report's central conclusion is that most AI-citation-earning behavior overlaps heavily with established SEO practice — summarized as "win SEO, win AI citations, most of the time, with extra steps" — rather than requiring an entirely separate GEO/AEO toolkit. The piece includes an evidence heat map, individual explanations for each of the 23 factors, and a companion downloadable "7-Step AI Citation Checklist."

## Why This Resource Matters
This report stands out in the AI-Powered SEO Content Production space because it is evidence-weighted rather than opinion-based — a rarity in a field often dominated by unverified "best practice" lists. By explicitly scoring factors on repeatability and evidence strength (and openly noting that correlation is not causation), it gives content producers and SEOs a rare, transparent methodology for prioritizing which AI-visibility tactics are actually worth investing in, making it a valuable primary reference for anyone building AI-citation-focused content strategy.
