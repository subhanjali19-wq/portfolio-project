## Summary
The AI Search Manual is iPullRank's flagship resource operationalizing Mike King's Relevance Engineering framework for practitioners. It is structured as an implementation hub rather than a single article, including downloadable tools, reporting templates, prompt recipes for GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) testing, and a glossary translating technical AI-search terminology for teams onboarding to the discipline.

The manual reflects iPullRank's broader positioning: that modern search optimization requires treating AI retrieval systems as engineering problems (vector embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation, passage-level relevance scoring) rather than purely content or keyword problems. It's explicitly built around Mike's assertion that iPullRank has delivered over $4 billion in organic search results for enterprise clients using this systems-based approach.

## Why This Resource Matters
For a research project on AI-powered SEO content production, this resource is valuable because it moves past high-level commentary into an actual operational toolkit — templates and testing recipes a team could apply directly, rather than only conceptual framing. It's a useful bridge between Mike's more abstract "Relevance Engineering" thought leadership (seen in his LinkedIn posts and interviews) and the concrete, repeatable processes a content production pipeline would need to implement his framework.
