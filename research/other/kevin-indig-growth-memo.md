## Summary
Growth Memo is Kevin Indig's weekly, research-backed newsletter on SEO, organic growth, and AI-driven search, read by tens of thousands of subscribers and described by readers as maintaining an unusually high signal-to-noise ratio for the SEO industry. Recent 2026 research collected in the newsletter includes large-sample studies such as an analysis of 815,000 query-page pairs on what wins ChatGPT citations, a 3,981-domain study across 115 prompts and 4 AI search engines on mention-versus-citation behavior, and a finding that 91% of AI citations appear in only one engine, meaning visibility does not reliably transfer across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

A representative recent issue, "Stop Trying to Replace People with AI," pushes back on industry narratives that AI will imminently replace entire job functions, citing Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index data on "Frontier Professionals" and referencing Klarna's public reversal after over-automating customer support and later admitting to quality declines.

## Why This Resource Matters
For a research project on AI-powered SEO content production, Growth Memo is valuable precisely because it treats AI content and AI search claims as empirical questions rather than settled conventional wisdom — publishing original data (query-pair analysis, citation-rate studies, engine-by-engine comparisons) instead of repeating vendor talking points. It's a strong counterbalance to more promotional AI-content-scaling narratives found elsewhere in this research set.
