## Summary
SEOFOMO is Aleyda Solís's weekly SEO newsletter, publicly cited across multiple sources as having 35,000–45,000+ subscribers. Each issue curates recent SEO and AI search news, original research, tool updates, and highlights specific practitioners worth following — often including other experts from this research repo's list, such as Lily Ray. Aleyda also runs companion newsletters (MarketingFOMO, AI Marketers) and free learning roadmaps (LearningSEO.io, LearningAIsearch.com) under the same editorial approach: curation grounded in what she sees directly through her own consulting work at Orainti, rather than secondhand commentary.

Because the newsletter is a recurring, dated-by-issue publication rather than a single article, no single "publication date" applies — a fact worth noting explicitly for this research repo rather than picking an arbitrary date.

## Why This Resource Matters
For a project on AI-powered SEO content production, SEOFOMO is useful less as a single source of insight and more as a filtering mechanism — it's a running record of what a highly plugged-in international SEO consultant judges to be signal versus noise in a fast-moving field. It's also a practical model of AI-adjacent content production itself: a high-frequency, curated, single-author newsletter format that has sustained a large subscriber base over multiple years without relying on AI-generated scale tactics.
