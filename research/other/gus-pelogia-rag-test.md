# Small Tests to Yield Big Answers on What Influences LLMs

**Type:** Blog / Column (Search Engine Land)
**Publication Date:** November 17, 2025
**URL:** https://searchengineland.com/small-tests-to-yield-big-answers-464222

## Summary
In this Search Engine Land column, Gus Pelogia moves past abstract, technical discussions of vector embeddings, passage ranking, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) by walking through a first-hand, reproducible experiment. Before a personal trip to Austin, Texas, he asked ChatGPT whether he had any upcoming travel; with no public information about the trip online, ChatGPT correctly reported no planned trips. He then published a blog post about the trip on his own site, and six hours later, ChatGPT's answer changed to confirm the (fictional, at the time of asking) trip — demonstrating that ChatGPT had used RAG to fetch fresh, up-to-date web content rather than relying solely on static training data.

He further details the timeline of how ChatGPT initially picked up just the blog post's title/snippet from his homepage before fully indexing the complete post content days later. From this, he draws a practical lesson for brands: since LLM answers are highly dependent on what is actually published on a website (especially for newer or smaller brands with limited existing information online), companies should use their own web pages deliberately to shape brand positioning and messaging that AI tools might surface — citing examples like Semrush's and Instagram's homepage taglines as intentional positioning statements that could influence how an LLM describes the brand.

## Why This Resource Matters
This article is a valuable, low-jargon entry point into understanding practical AI-powered SEO content production, because it replaces theoretical explanations of RAG with a transparent, personally-run test that readers can conceptually replicate. It directly demonstrates how quickly (within hours) LLMs can incorporate newly published content into their answers, reinforcing the strategic importance of timely publishing and clear on-page brand positioning as levers for AI search visibility — a core, actionable insight for anyone producing content intended to be picked up by AI systems like ChatGPT.
