## Transcript Summary
- Ross discusses how the SERP is shifting daily, with Google surpassing Reddit in some contexts while Reddit, LinkedIn, and local platforms like Yelp gain share in B2B and local search.
- He argues that SEO visibility increasingly comes from platforms beyond Google — Reddit, LinkedIn, and YouTube — where users research and build trust before purchasing.
- Distribution is framed as the mechanism that creates leverage: content performs when intentionally placed inside existing conversations, not simply published and left alone.
- Ross contends that holistic SEO now outperforms an onsite-only focus, since brand, PR, and social signals increasingly shape how AI systems evaluate relevance and credibility.
- He describes an industry split forming between SEOs who continue prior-year tactics and those evolving from a purely onsite mindset toward offsite, multi-channel distribution.
- Experimentation is emphasized as a competitive advantage: teams running low-risk technical tests alongside selective high-risk channel bets learn and adapt faster than competitors.
- Ross references his long-standing advocacy (since 2018) for brands to build presence on Yelp, Facebook, Reddit, Quora, and LinkedIn — arguing recent AI search trends validate that early thesis.
- He recommends prioritizing distribution channels based on where a brand's specific audience (e.g., B2B decision-makers) actually spends time, rather than chasing every emerging platform.
- The conversation closes with a preview of his SEO Week 2026 talk, continuing his focus on AI search, multi-platform discovery, and distribution strategy.
