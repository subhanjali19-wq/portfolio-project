# 202: Aleyda Solís — AI Search Crawlability and Why Your Site's Technical Foundations Decide Your Visibility

**Speaker:** Aleyda Solís (interviewed by Phil Gamache & Darrell Alfonso)  
**Publication Date:** January 13, 2026  
**YouTube URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s  
(Podcast: Humans of Martech)


## Transcript Summary
- AI crawlers behave differently from traditional search engine crawlers and frequently get blocked without site owners noticing.
- AI crawlers generally do not process JavaScript — they read raw markup, meaning sites relying on heavy client-side rendering can lose entire menus, product details, and pricing tables from AI visibility.
- Google eventually solved JavaScript rendering over time, but AI crawlers currently skip that rendering pipeline entirely, making simpler, server-rendered pages more reliably visible.
- Users now move fluidly across Google, TikTok, Instagram, and LLMs, so discovery needs to be treated as a multi-platform system rather than a single-channel strategy.
- She references OpenAI's published research showing a rise in task-oriented queries, where users ask models to complete goals directly rather than browsing page by page.
- Brand authority signals are shifting: mentions across Discord threads, YouTube comments, forums, and industry roundups now function as a reputation map that AI systems draw on as context, alongside traditional backlinks.
- Smaller/lesser-known brands can gain surprising AI visibility simply by showing up authentically in real conversations across these community spaces.
- Her core recommendations: verify your actual AI visibility (don't assume), publish content grounded in real customer language, and use topic clusters as an anchor strategy since AI prompts scatter across many phrasings.
