# BrightonSEO: Gus Pelogia over het Model Context Protocol

**Speaker:** Gus Pelogia (Senior SEO Product Manager, Indeed), interviewed on the SEO Cast podcast/video series at BrightonSEO
**Publication Date:** Approximate 2026
**YouTube URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8IgJrxsoaqA

## Transcript Summary
* The interview (conducted in Dutch, with Gus responding in English) covers how the Model Context Protocol (MCP) connects data from SEO tools like Ahrefs and Semrush directly to LLMs.
* Gus explains MCP as effectively "connecting live SEO tool APIs to LLMs" — a way to let AI assistants like Claude query live SEO data instead of guessing or hallucinating answers.
* He discusses automated keyword research workflows, such as filtering large keyword sets (e.g., "high-paying jobs" combined with job titles) using natural-language prompts instead of manual spreadsheet work.
* He addresses current limitations of SERP APIs accessed via MCP, including gaps in retrieving certain data types (e.g., paid/ad-supported SERP views) compared to a tool's native UI.
* He emphasizes that MCP and AI automation are not replacements for SEO judgment, but tools that free up time previously spent on repetitive, recurring tasks.
* He touches on the importance of user testing for landing pages as part of a broader SXO (Search Experience Optimization) approach that blends technical SEO, UX, and conversion.
* He discusses cross-team collaboration between SEO, engineering, and product functions when implementing AI-driven SEO tooling at scale (drawing on his experience at Indeed).
* The conversation situates MCP servers within the broader 2026 wave of SEO tool vendors (Ahrefs, Semrush, SE Ranking, DataForSEO, Screaming Frog) releasing official LLM connectors.
