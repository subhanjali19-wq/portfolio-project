# Google Will Kill Your Traffic – Here's How You Adapt | Kevin Indig (Growth Memo)

**Speaker:** Kevin Indig (interviewed by Tim Soulo)
**Publication Date:** August 26, 2025
**YouTube URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQXvbeYF5go
(Ahrefs Podcast)

---

## Transcript Summary
- Kevin analyzed over 5 billion search visits and found people are searching more often but spending less time per session, with zero-click searches on the rise.
- He argues SEO is not dying but entering a new investment cycle, with resources shifting toward AI visibility rather than traditional ranking.
- The conversation covers how AI Overviews are changing user behavior on the SERP itself, not just diverting clicks elsewhere.
- Kevin discusses the "evergreen content" model (guides, explainers, tutorials) as increasingly less effective in an AI-answer-first environment.
- He describes a shift in focus from ranking position to "influence" — presence and citation across ChatGPT, Reddit, YouTube, and other platforms beyond Google itself.
- The discussion addresses how classic mid-funnel tracking (time on page, multi-page browsing behavior) is eroding as users get fuller answers directly inside AI tools.
- Kevin recommends companies start evaluating which site elements are AI-agent-unfriendly and begin tracking AI crawler/agent activity in server logs.
- Closing discussion touches on how this shift blurs organizational lines between SEO, PR, social, and brand teams, requiring more cross-functional coordination.
