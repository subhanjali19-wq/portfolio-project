# Tough Questions CEOs are Asking About AI SEO (and How To Answer Them) w/ Gaetano DiNardi

**Speaker:** Gaetano DiNardi (Growth Advisor, Marketing Advice), interviewed by Ross Hudgens (Founder & CEO, Siege Media)
**Publication Date:** December 1, 2025
**YouTube URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=16aSswhc4PM

## Transcript Summary
* Gaetano and Ross discuss the tough questions CEOs are now asking SEO teams as traffic goes flat or declines in the AI search era.
* They argue traffic is no longer a reliable primary health metric for SEO, since AI Overviews and LLM answers break the traditional "rank → CTR → traffic → leads → revenue" model.
* Gaetano recommends CMOs shift focus from raw sessions to demo requests, pipeline contribution, deal size, and overall organic-channel performance.
* He describes reframing SEO strategy for mid-market B2B SaaS companies by shifting investment away from decaying top-of-funnel informational content toward bottom-of-funnel "money pages."
* He introduces the concept of optimizing for LLM citations and "money prompts" — high-intent queries where being cited or recommended by an AI system directly influences purchase decisions.
* He discusses "crash-the-party" comparison pages as a tactic for capturing high-intent demand across both traditional Google search and AI-driven platforms.
* The conversation addresses how to communicate these shifts to CEOs and executives who are used to judging SEO purely by traffic volume.
* Overall framing: SEO's value proposition must evolve from a traffic-generation channel to a pipeline- and revenue-attribution channel in the AI search era.
