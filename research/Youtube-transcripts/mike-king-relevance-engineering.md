## Transcript Summary
- Mike explains that iPullRank was experimenting with language models and building tools that mimicked AI Overviews as early as 2020, using GPT-2, well before ChatGPT existed.
- He describes moving from optimizing full pages to optimizing at the passage level, framing this shift as central to "relevance engineering."
- He argues SEOs are no longer just "mechanics tweaking websites" but are becoming engineers building entire systems.
- Mike details his own content strategy process: starting from deep audience analysis (needs, motivations, emotional triggers) and working downward to entities and specific keywords, rather than starting from massive keyword lists.
- He discusses using tools like Qforia (built at iPullRank) and third-party query fan-out simulators to approach AI Mode's query fan-out process systematically.
- He explains Google's AI Mode/AI Overviews mechanics in terms of a single query expanding into a broader fan-out set that generates a corpus of source documents.
- He argues SEOs need to move beyond blog posts into white papers and original research to remain competitive in an AI-answer environment.
- He touches on the rise of "agentic SEO" — automated, modular systems that adapt to visibility shifts — and newer success metrics like branded visibility, mindshare, and directional traffic signals, replacing pure click/ranking metrics.
